What this is

This is a safe place for women to tell their stories anonymously. Each story helps another woman see that she is believed and that she is not the only one.

With the writer’s permission, stories are added to our story library, where anyone can read them and search by topic or keyword.

How it works

  1. 1 Write your story. Type it here or upload a file. Share as much or as little as you want. Leaving your email is optional.
  2. 2 Choose what happens to it. Let us share it anonymously in the library, or keep it private so only our team reads it.
  3. 3 We read every story with care. A person reads each story and removes names and details that could identify you or anyone else.
  4. 4 It joins the story library. If you said yes, your story is published under your alias or “Jane Doe,” where other women can find it.

    Our privacy promise

    • Your story is anonymous. We don’t ask for your name, and we don’t record where you are writing from.
    • You decide if it’s shared. Your story is only added to the library if you say yes. You can ask us to take it down at any time by emailing iamjanedoestories@gmail.com.
    • Your email stays private. If you leave one, we use it only to follow up with you. It is never published, shared, or sold.
    • Every story is reviewed first. A person reads each story before anything is shared, and removes details that could identify you or anyone else.
